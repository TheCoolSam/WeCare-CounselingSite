THE METHODOLOGY

Your Healing Journey

Therapy is not a straight diagnostic line — it is a continuous spiral of self-realization and healing. Here is the structured pathway we navigate together toward clinical restoration.

01
STAGE 1 / Planting the Seed

Exploration🌱

We begin by creating a secure, judgment-free space where you can share your story. This initial phase is about understanding where you are, what brought you here, and establishing a trusted clinical alliance.

01

Focus Core Approaches

  • Initial clinical assessment
  • Building trust and secure rapport
  • Identifying behavioral patterns
  • Co-designing therapeutic goals
Milestone Accomplished

A felt sense of being deeply heard and understood, with clear objectives for your healing journey.

02
STAGE 2 / Taking Root

🌿Connection

As trust deepens, we venture into the core of your challenges. You'll cultivate deep self-awareness around how past narratives influence your present behaviors and learn active somatic/cognitive tools.

02

Focus Core Approaches

  • Exploring core formative causes
  • Somatic coping strategies
  • Challenging rigid belief cycles
  • Processing difficult emotions safely
Milestone Accomplished

Tangible shifts in how you relate to stress, trigger points, and your inner dialogue.

03
STAGE 3 / Reaching Upward

Growth🌳

This is the active phase of integration. You are daily practicing new skills, expanding comfort zones, and establishing healthy relationship structures. Growth during this stage feels nonlinear and dynamic.

03

Focus Core Approaches

  • Implementing new behavioral choices
  • Building emotional resilience
  • Strengthening relational boundaries
  • Integrating insights in real time
Milestone Accomplished

Measurable improvements in daily emotional stability, confidence, and interpersonal connections.

04
STAGE 4 / Bearing Fruit

🍃Transformation

You have integrated the therapeutic process into your identity. The self-awareness and clinical tools you have gained are now second-nature, transitioning our relationship toward completion.

04

Focus Core Approaches

  • Consolidating personal growth gains
  • Planning for future vulnerabilities
  • Celebrating narrative transformation
  • Transitioning to clinical maintenance
Milestone Accomplished

Full emotional independence, feeling deeply equipped to navigate life with profound self-compassion and wisdom.
